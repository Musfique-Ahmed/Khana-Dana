🍴 About Us

Who We Are

At Khana Dana, we believe that the best food is made at home. Our snacks are prepared by skilled home chefs using fresh ingredients and traditional Bengali recipes — ensuring every bite brings you warmth, comfort, and nostalgia.

Our Promise:

  • 100% Homemade
  • Hygienically Prepared
  • Frozen Fresh for Maximum Taste

Our Story:

Founded with love for Bengali street snacks, Khana Dana started as a small kitchen idea and grew into an online brand serving delicious, homemade frozen snacks that you can enjoy without the hassle of preparation.
