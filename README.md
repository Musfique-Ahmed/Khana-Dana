# Khana Dana 🍴

**Homemade Bengali Snacks, Frozen Fresh & Ready to Fry!**

## About

Khana Dana is a takeaway restaurant specializing in authentic Bengali snacks. We offer homemade delicacies like samucha, shingara, rolls, and puri - all made with love and care, just like home.

### What Makes Us Special

- **100% Homemade**: Every snack is prepared using traditional Bengali recipes
- **Frozen Fresh**: Delivered frozen to lock in freshness and flavor
- **Ready to Fry**: Simply heat and serve for crispy, golden perfection
- **Hygienic**: Prepared with the highest standards of food safety
- **Nostalgic Taste**: Full of authentic flavors that bring back memories of home

## Menu

- **Samucha** - Crispy triangular pastries filled with spiced potatoes and peas
- **Shingara** - Traditional Bengali samosas with a perfect blend of spices
- **Rolls** - Delicious rolls with various fillings wrapped in soft paratha
- **Puri** - Light, fluffy, and crispy deep-fried bread
